THREE EXTREME ENVIRONMENTS

EARTH ANALOGS

Explore three remarkable places that help us understand selected conditions beyond Earth.

CHILE 01

EXTREME ARID ENVIRONMENT

Atacama Desert

One of the driest places on Earth. Its extreme aridity and soil conditions make it valuable for studying selected aspects of Mars exploration.

Extreme dryness Soil research Mars analog
ANTARCTICA 02

POLAR DESERT ENVIRONMENT

McMurdo Dry Valleys

A cold, dry Antarctic landscape with exposed rock, frozen ground and unusual water systems. It offers insights into polar and cold-desert processes.

Extreme cold Ice and water Polar research
HAWAII 03

VOLCANIC HIGH-ALTITUDE TERRAIN

Mauna Kea

A high volcanic mountain with rocky terrain and volcanic materials. Its landscape can support research into geology and exploration operations.

Volcanic geology High altitude Terrain research

IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC NOTE

EARTH IS NOT ANOTHER PLANET.

Earth analogs are useful research environments, but none of them perfectly reproduces the Moon or Mars.

01

Gravity Earth's gravity is different from lunar and Martian gravity.

02

Atmosphere The Moon has almost no atmosphere, while Mars has a very thin atmosphere.

03

Radiation Earth's magnetic field and atmosphere provide protection that is not available in the same way on the Moon and Mars.
